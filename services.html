01 / Approach

Behavioral science.
Better experiences.

Understanding what people do is only part of the picture. Understanding why they do it is where research becomes especially valuable.

My approach combines behavioral science, systematic observation, qualitative research, and data analysis to help identify barriers, understand user decisions, and uncover opportunities for improvement.

I offer independent, remote research services for game developers, product teams, and organizations seeking a clearer understanding of the people they serve.
